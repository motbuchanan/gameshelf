⌂ Shelf
📝 How-To Guides

📱 Put the shelf on your home screen

  1. Open the shelf in Chrome on your phone.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right) → Add to Home screen (sometimes shown as Install app).
  3. Tap Add. The Game Shelf icon appears with your other apps.

It launches full-screen like a real app — no browser bars.

iPhone: open in Safari, tap the Share square → Add to Home Screen.

👥 Players & profiles

Open the 👥 Players book on the shelf to set up everyone who plays.

  1. Add a player: give them a name, a token color, and a look — their initial, a game icon (dice, crown, paw…), or even a photo.
  2. The owner: the first time the shelf opens on a phone, it asks who owns the device. That's just so it knows whose settings to use — everyone who plays is still tracked.
  3. See stats: tap any player to see their wins and games.
  4. Remove a player: tap them, then Remove. It asks first, and it clears that player's stats.
A player's color and icon (or photo) show up on the “who goes first” screen when a game starts.

🔄 Share players & records across devices

On the shelf footer, tap Sync this device to the household and enter your family code. Phones that share a code share the same players and the same Record Book — play on the tablet, see it on the phone. (Sync shares the roster and scores, not a live game in progress.)

🧾 Pass-and-play

Games for two or more pass the one phone around. Between turns a curtain hides the screen so the next player can pick it up without peeking — tap I'm ready when it's your go. Card and hidden-info games (Go Fish, One Card Left!, Codebreaker, Battleship, Guess Who) all keep your hand secret this way.

🤖 Playing against the computer

Many games can fill a seat with a computer player:

  1. Games with a vs Computer button (Checkers, Reversi, Trouble, Connect 4, Mancala, Dots & Boxes, Snakes & Ladders, War, Royal Game of Ur) cycle Easy → Medium → Hard.
  2. Chess goes further: levels 1–10 plus a 🎓 Coach that gives hints, warns before blunders, and allows takebacks.
  3. One Card Left! can fill any empty seat with a computer — play solo against up to three of them.
Start anyone new on Easy — it makes honest mistakes on purpose. Hard means it.

📖 The Rulebook

The 📖 The Rulebook book on the shelf holds full, illustrated rules for every game — goals, how pieces move, the clever bits, and house tips. Inside a game, tap the ? (Rules) button to read that game's rules without leaving your seat.

🃏 One Card Left! & house rules

Match the top card by color or number, play action cards (Skip, Reverse, Draw Two) and wilds, and be first to empty your hand. When you're down to one card the table calls it for you.

Before you tap Start, you can switch on the Stacking house rule: answer a Draw card with another Draw card and pile the total onto the next player instead of taking it yourself.

🧩 Friendly games

On the Who's-playing screen, tap ✓ Counts for the record to toggle a friendly match — useful for teaching or testing. Friendly games never touch the stats. Backing out with ⟨ Shelf never counts either; only finished games go in the Record Book.

🔍 Easy view

On the shelf, toggle 🔍 Easy view to make text and tap targets bigger across every game — great for young players, older eyes, or when the phone sits in the middle of the table. It's remembered per device.

📺 TV mode

Toggle 📺 TV mode when the phone lies flat on the table or is cast to a shared screen. Games that normally spin the board to face each player (Sorry!, Checkers, Backgammon, Chinese Checkers) hold still instead. Set it before opening the game.

🔊 Sound on / off

Every game has a 🔊 Sound button near the bottom (in Sorry! it's in the menu). One tap mutes or unmutes everything, and your choice is remembered. If sound seems missing, tap the screen once first — phones keep audio asleep until you touch them.

🔀 Sorting the shelf

The 🔀 Sort button cycles Shelf (the curated order) → Most played (this device's real favorites) → A–Z. It remembers your pick.

🔍 Zooming the big boards

On Scrabble and Bridge Builders, pinch the board to zoom (up to 3×), drag to slide around while zoomed, and double-tap to jump in or out. Tiles land exactly where you tap.

🎮 Play with a Bluetooth controller

Any Bluetooth game controller works — Xbox, PlayStation, or generic.

  1. Pair the controller in your phone or tablet's Bluetooth settings (the app can't pair it — that's a system thing).
  2. Open the Game Shelf and press any button on the controller. A gold ring appears.
  3. Use the d-pad or stick to move the ring, A to select, B to go back.

Right now the controller steers the shelf and all the menus and buttons. Tap the 🎮 Controller pill on the shelf any time for these steps and a live “connected” check.

Playing moves right on the game boards by controller is rolling out game by game — and you'll be able to pick your input (touch or controller) per player.

✈️ Play with no internet

After you've opened the shelf a couple of times, every game and both puzzle books work offline — road trips, flights, basements. Scores you earn offline stay on the phone, and household sync catches up automatically when the signal returns.
