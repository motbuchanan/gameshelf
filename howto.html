⌂ Shelf
📝 How-To Guides

📱 Put the shelf on your home screen

  1. Open the shelf in Chrome on your phone.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right) → Add to Home screen (sometimes shown as Install app).
  3. Tap Add. The Game Shelf icon appears with your other apps.

It launches full-screen like a real app — no browser bars.

iPhone: open in Safari, tap the Share square → Add to Home Screen.

✈️ Play with no internet

After you've visited the shelf a couple of times, every game works offline — road trips, flights, basements. Scores you earn offline are kept on the phone, and the household sync catches up automatically when the signal returns.

🔊 Sound on / off

Every game has a 🔊 Sound button near the bottom (in Sorry! it lives in the menu). One tap mutes or unmutes everything, and the shelf remembers your choice. If sound seems missing, tap anywhere once first — phones keep audio asleep until the first touch.

📺 TV mode

On the shelf, toggle 📺 TV mode when the phone lies flat on the table or is cast to a screen everyone can see. Games that normally spin the board to face each player (Sorry!, Checkers, Backgammon, Chinese Checkers) will hold still instead. Toggle it before opening the game.

🤖 Playing against the computer

Trouble, Checkers, Reversi, and Royal Game of Ur have a vs Computer button: tap it to cycle Easy → Medium → Hard. The computer takes the second player's seat. Easy is friendly and beatable; Hard means it.

Start anyone new on Easy — it makes honest mistakes on purpose.

🔎 Zooming the big boards

On Scrabble and Bridge Builders, pinch the board to zoom in (up to 3×), drag to slide around while zoomed, and double-tap to jump in or out instantly. Tiles land exactly where you tap — no more fat-finger bounce-backs.

🔄 Sorting the shelf

The 🔀 Sort button cycles Shelf (the curated order) → Most played (this device's real favorites) → A–Z. It remembers your pick.

🧩 Friendly games

On the Who's-playing screen, tap ✓ Counts for the record to toggle a friendly match — useful for teaching games or testing. Friendly games never touch the stats. Backing out with ⟨ Shelf never counts either; only finished games go in the Record Book.

🎮 Hook up a Bluetooth controller

Pairing a controller to your phone and steering the shelf with it — the guide lands here when the feature does.
