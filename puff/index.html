Practice Room

Try every move and every power. Nothing here can end your game. If your health runs out, you pop back out of the door.

Moves

◀ ▶
Walk. Tap twice fast, or keep holding, to run.
JUMP
Jump. Press it again in the air to float, and keep tapping to fly higher.
B
Inhale. Then ▼ to swallow, or B to spit a star.
Copy
Swallow an enemy that has a power and it becomes yours. B uses it.
DROP
Lets a power go as a star. Inhale the star to get it back.
▼ + JUMP
Slide kick.
GUARD
Hold it to block.
Balloon
From a Bloon. Tap B to puff up. Each tap makes you bigger, a little faster, and bouncier. A fourth tap pops you and stuns every enemy nearby.
Sleep
From a Noddy. B takes a nap, then you wake up with 3 health back.
Krackl
The storm cloud at the end of Night. He drops lightning (watch him glow first) and rain, and swoops low across the floor. Duck under the swoop, or jump on him, or inhale his hail and spit it at him while he is low.
Food
Heals you. The big tomato heals everything.
▲
Go through a door. The doors in the Practice room open the World Map. Every world has three levels: Explore (find the stars), the Gauntlet (ends at a mini-boss), and the Boss. Beat a level to open the next one, beat the boss to win that world's Dream Star and open the next world.
Map
The Map button opens the World Map any time. Pick any level you have opened.
Book
Puff's Book fills in a card for every enemy and boss the first time you inhale or beat it, and keeps a shelf of badges you have earned.
Flags
Touch the flag in a Gauntlet and it turns gold. If your health runs out, you come back at the flag.
Mini-bosses
Chunk charges and gets dizzy when he hits the wall. Bonk hops and shakes the floor. Noddy Prime snores out rings. Rock Roller rolls wall to wall and gets dizzy. Jump on them or spit stars at them.
Rumble
The crystal golem at the end of the Cave. He stomps (shockwaves and crystal shards fall) and throws shards. Inhale a shard for the Stone power, then drop on him.
Stars
Gold stars are hidden around every level. The ones you find are remembered. Each level door shows how many you have found, like 3/8. Collect all 8 in a level to finish it.
Secret
Each level hides one glowing gold star door. Climb up and find it to reach the Star Vault, a room packed with stars.
Platforms
The glowing purple platforms float back and forth and up and down. Hop on and ride them. Hold ▼ to drop off.

Who has which power

  • Fire breather: Fire
  • Snowman: Ice, freezes enemies into blocks you can kick
  • Spark blob: Spark
  • Falling rock: Stone, press B again to change back
  • Sword knight: Sword
  • One-eyed walker: Beam
  • Bloon (floats): Balloon
  • Noddy (sleepy): Sleep

World Map
